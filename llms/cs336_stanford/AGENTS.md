# Transcript notes

Write notes from the transcript, keeping only information that helps explain the lecture or supports later study. Avoid filler, repeated points, generic background, and unsupported additions.

Do not compress useful explanations into bare conclusions. Include enough context, reasoning, and examples to make important ideas understandable on their own. When the transcript contains substantial material, notes can be around 150 lines; use the amount of detail the source supports instead of forcing every note to the same length. If only a few points are useful, keep it short.

Preserve important distinctions, caveats, formulas, and concrete examples from the speaker. Paraphrase clearly; do not reproduce transcript chatter, introductions, jokes, or administrative details unless they affect the course material.
